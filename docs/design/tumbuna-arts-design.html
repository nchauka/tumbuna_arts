Tumbuna
Arts

Buy PNG Art.Discover Its Story.Preserve Its Culture.

Tumbuna is Tok Pisin for ancestors. Tumbuna Arts is a mobile marketplace and living archive for Papua New Guinean carvers, weavers, bilum makers and painters. Every listing ships with its story, its province, its materials and a QR-verifiable certificate of authenticity.

01 · Branding

A marketplace with an elder's memory

The brand sits between a premium shop and a cultural institution. Commerce is red and confident. Heritage is earth brown and unhurried. Trust is gold. The bilum's zigzag ties them together.

Positioning

The only place to buy PNG art where the maker, the meaning and the authenticity come with the object. Etsy's ease of buying, with the depth of a museum label.

Brand pillars

  • Authentic. Verified makers, certified works.
  • Storied. Culture is content, not decoration.
  • Fair. Artists see every kina they earn.
  • Proud. Made in PNG, shown to the world.

Voice

Warm, plain and respectful. Use the maker's own words and Tok Pisin where it belongs ("Moning", "Tenkyu tru"), always with English beside it. Never call work "tribal" or "primitive"; name the people, place and technique instead.

Logo concepts

Recommended · Primary

A · Bilum T

Two looped zigzag rows form the crossbar of a T, and a column of diamonds forms the stem: the exact motif of a looped bilum strap. Reads at 16px, works as the app icon, splash and favicon.

Alternate · Heritage sub-brand

B · Tumbuna face

An ancestor face drawn from the shared vocabulary of carved masks: ringed eyes, long nose, chevron brow. Used for the Heritage archive tab and printed tags. Kept generic so it copies no single clan design.

System mark · Verification

C · Kundu seal

An hourglass kundu drum inside a ringed seal. It appears only where authenticity is claimed: certificates, QR tags, the verified badge's large form. Its scarcity is what makes it mean something.

App icon & wordmark

Tumbuna·Arts

Wordmark: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, −3% tracking, red interpunct. Clear space equals the height of one diamond. Minimum mark size 20px.

Brand textures

Bilum zigzag (brand moments, 6–10px bands), wood grain (heritage headers), tapa rings (empty states), basket weave (packaging). Textures frame photography and never sit behind body text.

02 · Color palette

Flag colors, carved-wood neutrals

The five brand colors come from the PNG flag and kwila-wood carving. Each has one job, so color alone tells a user where they are: red means act, gold means verified, brown means heritage.

Dark mode

Night-market dark: warm charcoal, never pure black. Red lifts to #EF4B4B for 4.5:1 contrast.

Accessibility

  • Ink on white 16.8:1; red on white 4.9:1 (AA for text ≥ 14px bold).
  • Gold is never text on white; use #9A6F00 (5.1:1).
  • White on red buttons 4.9:1.
  • Touch targets ≥ 44pt.
03 · Typography

Hand-cut display, clear reading text

Bricolage Grotesque has the slightly irregular, chiselled feel of carved lettering without being a novelty face. Manrope is calm and readable for long stories and dense listings. 04 · Design system & UI style guide

Components that let the art lead

White canvas, generous photography, soft 16px corners on interactive objects, pills for filters and badges. Shadows are warm-tinted and used only on things that float: sticky bars, sheets and the cart button.

Buttons

Height 52, radius 16, Manrope ExtraBold 15. One primary (red) per screen. Primary sits in the bottom thumb zone, full width, in a sticky bar on commerce screens.

Imagery

Natural daylight, plain sand or white backdrop, object fills 70% of frame. Minimum 4 photos: front, back, detail, scale (in hand). Optional 15-second video of the maker at work.

Motion

150ms for taps, 250ms for screen transitions, 400ms for the certificate reveal. Shared-element transition from card image to product hero. Springs (damping 18) for sheets. Reduced-motion users get fades.

Empty & loading

Skeletons in sand100 that keep the 4:5 card shape. Empty states use the tapa texture with one line and one action: "Your wishlist is empty. Explore bilums from Goroka."

05 · Icon set

Rounded line icons, one weight

Use Phosphor Icons (phosphor-react-native) in the Regular weight at 24px, 2px stroke and round joins, with Fill weight for selected tabs. On iOS, expo-symbols SF Symbols can replace the tab icons. Three custom icons cover what no set has: the verified seal, the bilum (archive) and the kundu (certificate).

07 · Mobile user flows

Three journeys the product must nail

Buyer: discover to delivered

Target: first purchase in under 3 minutes, guest checkout allowed.

flowchart TD
  a[Home feed] --> b[Artwork card]
  b --> c[Product details]
  c -. optional .-> d[Read story / watch maker video]
  c --> e[Add to cart]
  e --> f[Cart: shipping estimate]
  f --> g[Checkout 1: address]
  g --> h[Checkout 2: shipping + export check]
  h --> i[Checkout 3: pay]
  i --> j[Order confirmed]
  j --> k[Track: packed, shipped, delivered]
  k --> l[Certificate saved to profile]
  l --> m[Leave review]

Artist: register to first payout

Designed for low bandwidth: drafts save offline, photos upload in the background.

flowchart TD
  a[Register as artist] --> b[Profile: name, province, craft]
  b --> c[Verification: ID + referee + 3 works]
  c --> d{Review 48h}
  d -- approved --> e[Gold verified badge]
  d -- more info --> c
  b --> f[Upload artwork, 4 steps]
  f --> g[Draft visible only to artist]
  e --> h[Listing goes live with certificate]
  g --> h
  h --> i[Order notification]
  i --> j[Print label, mark shipped]
  j --> k[Buyer confirms delivery]
  k --> l[Payout to bank or mobile money]

Anyone: verify authenticity

flowchart LR
  a[Scan tag QR] --> b{Serial on ledger?}
  b -- yes, photos match --> c[Authentic: open certificate]
  b -- yes, already transferred --> d[Show ownership history]
  b -- no --> e[Not recognised: report listing]
  c --> f[Artist + story + province]

Flow principles

  • Browse without an account. Ask to sign in at "Add to wishlist" or checkout, never before.
  • Story is one tap from every artwork, never forced before buying.
  • Show totals in Kina with a live USD/AUD estimate for international buyers.
  • Flag export permits early: listings that need a National Museum & Art Gallery export permit show it on the product page, not as a surprise at payment.
  • Artists can do everything on a mid-range Android phone on 3G.
